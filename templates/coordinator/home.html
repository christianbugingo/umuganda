A simple way to work together

The platform connects residents who want to help with the local leaders organizing community work. It brings project information into one place, while keeping announcements distinct from volunteer sign-ups.

1. Find work nearby

Browse upcoming projects and narrow results by province, district, sector, cell, village, or date. Project details include the purpose, time, location, and number of volunteers needed.

2. Join as a volunteer

Create a volunteer account, choose a project, and sign up. Your dashboard keeps your registrations and attendance history together.

3. Coordinate local work

Approved leaders can publish and update projects for their managed area, and share separate community notices or opportunities with residents.

4. Keep information current

Leaders can correct or remove their own project details and announcements. Past projects stop appearing in public project listings.

Built around Rwanda’s community structure

Umuganda is shared community work: people contribute time and effort to improve the places where they live. This platform supports that tradition with a clear place to discover activities, coordinate participation, and communicate local information.

Location filters follow the administrative hierarchy—province, district, sector, cell, and village—so residents can look for activities close to home. Leaders describe the area they manage when applying; an administrator must verify and approve leader accounts before they can publish.

The platform helps organize information, but it does not replace local authorities or verify real-world appointments automatically. Community leaders remain responsible for keeping the information they publish accurate and current.
